# Brand assets

Downloaded 2026-09-15 from the [XCMG corporate website](https://www.xcmg.com/).

- `xcmg-logo.png`: unmodified white wordmark, [official image](https://www.xcmg.com/upload/images/2023/12/25/1ae901c2427a4bf9806eaaf5c685ffd7.png), 500 × 110 pixels.
- `icon16.png`, `icon32.png`, `icon48.png`, `icon128.png`: size-specific exports from the 106 px mark in the official wordmark above. `scripts/generate_extension_icons.py` preserves its geometry, applies the existing XCMG blue, and keeps the white openings opaque for light and dark Chrome themes.


XCMG names and marks belong to their respective owner. These assets identify the equipment ecosystem in this competition project; they are not covered by this repository's software license. The assistant is an independent competition prototype, not an official XCMG service.
